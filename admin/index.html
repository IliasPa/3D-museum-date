      🪞 The mirror

      From far away it's black. Standing in front of it, it swirls and turns into the thing you want the most: how you dream to be in the future, or how things would have been if you'd never met. Every person sees their own pictures (with several, one is picked at random each time — the same one may come again). Without a picture for someone, the mirror shows the two of you, reflected.
